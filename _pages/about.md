<h1>About me</h1>

I am currently an MA student in the <a href="https://www.mcgill.ca/linguistics" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Department of Linguistics</a> at <a href="https://www.mcgill.ca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">McGill University</a> (Montréal, QC, Canada). Prior to this, I was an undergraduate in the <a href="https://www.smu.ca/linguistics/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Program in Linguistics</a> at <a href="https://www.smu.ca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Saint Mary's University</a> (Halifax, NS, Canada).

I am interested in the second language acquisition of syntax and in experimental methods for studying linguistic data. My MA Research Paper examines the distribution and interpretation of certain Vietnamese constructions with <i>là</i>, especially their relationship to clefts, exhaustivity, and information structure at the syntax–semantics interface. My undergraduate Honours Thesis focuses on the interaction between prosody and syntactic ambiguity resolution in English relative clause attachment by L1 Vietnamese–L2 English bilinguals. I am also drawn to sociolinguistics, especially how language is used and talked about online.
