<h1>About me</h1>

I recently completed my MA in Linguistics at <a href="https://www.mcgill.ca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">McGill University</a> (Montréal, QC, Canada). My MA Research Paper examined the distribution and interpretation of certain Vietnamese constructions with <i>là</i>, especially their relationship to clefts, exhaustivity, and information structure at the syntax–semantics interface.

Before pursuing my MA, I earned an Honours BA in Linguistics with a Minor in Psychology at at <a href="https://www.smu.ca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Saint Mary's University</a> (Halifax, NS, Canada). My Honours Thesis investigated the interaction between prosody and syntactic ambiguity resolution in English relative clause attachment by L1 Vietnamese–L2 English bilinguals.

My research interests include second language acquisition, syntax, and experimental approaches to the study of linguistic data. I am particularly interested in the acquisition and processing of syntactic structure, as well as the syntax–semantics interface. I am also interested in sociolinguistics, especially language use and metalinguistic discourse in online communities.
